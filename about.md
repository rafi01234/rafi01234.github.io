Hey! I'm Raf, a cyber threat specialist who has been in the industry since ~2020, starting as an apprentice way back when and working my way up to threat intelligence lead.

I've been meaning to start this site for a while now, but ~~procrastination~~ *life* kept getting in the way.

I plan to use this site to post about threat intelligence- and cyber security-related research that I stumble upon in my day-to-day that I find particularly interesting or exciting, as well as to document some of my learning journeys, and the occasional rambling.

I'll try to maintain some form of cadence, but can't guarantee it. Burnout is real, and life takes priority.

Hopefully some people can find it useful. If not, I'll just be talking to myself, and that's OK too.

If you have any questions, please feel free to reach out (email in footer).

Raf.
